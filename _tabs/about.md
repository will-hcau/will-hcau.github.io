Hi, I’m Will — a software engineer currently based in Tokyo.

I studied Computer Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, and my career has since taken me from Hong Kong to Shenzhen and now Japan. Along the way, I’ve had the chance to work on all kinds of products, including media devices, drones, cameras, and PlayStation.

I enjoy learning how things work, solving problems, and turning ideas into something practical. This blog is a place for me to keep track of what I learn and share technical notes, small projects, useful discoveries, and the occasional wrong turn along the way.

Outside of work, I enjoy photography, travelling, and discovering new places — preferably somewhere with a good view and a camera nearby.

You can also find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/willhcau) or [GitHub](https://github.com/will-hcau).

Thanks for stopping by!
